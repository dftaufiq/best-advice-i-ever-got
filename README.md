One sentence about the best advice I ever received — a neighbour's line on the day my father died, and the cooking that followed.

> The day my father died, my neighbour brought soup and said, 'Grief is just love with nowhere to go — give it somewhere,' and I have been cooking for other people's bad days ever since.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`) and prints it
to stdout. The line is 184 characters — inside the 200-character cap.
